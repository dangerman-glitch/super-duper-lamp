Dangerman OWL · YouTube ad creative · shoot list

Three Ads,
One Session

Shoot all three back to back. Same outfit, same platform, same light — about twenty minutes. Every line is written to be said, not read, so change any word that doesn't sound like you.

00 Before you hit record

Framing Shoot 9:16 vertical. Stand left of center, bar in frame right. # crops cleanly to 16:9 later; shooting wide first never does Camera Phone, tripod, chest height, 3–4 feet back. Rear lens. Light Face the brightest window or open door. Nothing behind you brighter than your face. Audio Wired earbuds with a mic, or stand close. Gym reverb kills watch time faster than anything you say. Wardrobe Whatever you actually coach in. No logo-free blank tee — people buy from a coach who looks like he was mid-session. Set Loaded bar on the platform behind you. Chalk out. Don't tidy up. Takes Three per script, straight through. Pick in the edit, not on set.

One rule that matters more than the rest: look at the lens, not the screen. On a phone that means the little glass circle, not your own face. An ad where the coach looks slightly off-camera reads as a man talking about you. Looking down the lens reads as a man talking to you.
